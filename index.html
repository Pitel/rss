How to use fire to destroy thorns in Crimson Desert The easiest way to destroy thorns in Crimson Desert is to deal fire damage with either fire arrows or the Blinding Flash skill. Let's cover the skill first. Step one is to equip a sword. Press and hold the Ctrl and LMB at the same time. You can now use the skill by releasing and holding the Ctrl button. You'll unlock Blinding Flash pretty early in the game. It's part of an opening puzzle that has to be solved to open a locked door. If you're not using a sword, you use fire arrows. First, you have to find standard wooden arrows, which are relatively easy to find. Next, find a source of fire. Draw the bow, and touch the arrow's tip with the fire. This will set the arrow ablaze. Aim at the thorns to shoot the arrow, and this will clear your path. I prefer using Blinding Flash, as you might not always have a source of fire readily available while you're out and about.
