Screenshot by Destructoid When you break a light source, there will be a chance to find a floor chicken from it. When you consume this item, it will restore 10 HP. Screenshot by Destructoid You might also see mega stacks of floor chickens very rarely in food stalls, as shown in the image above. Finally, there's the Recovery Power Up that helps to restore your lost health. This Power Up restores one HP after an encounter. If you upgrade it three times (max), you'll get +3 HP after every battle. The number will feel less, but here's how you can use it efficiently. Once you take a hit on your HP, don't go immediately into another boss battle. Instead, try the normal battles and avoid taking more hits. This will help you restore your HP and also allow you to gain Gold and XP. At this rate, you can restore 9-12 HP quite easily, and effectively make the most out of the Recovery Power Up.
