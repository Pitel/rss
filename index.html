CloverPit Luck, explained The odds for getting symbols are displayed on a table to the left of the slot machine. Screenshot by Destructoid The Luck stat in CloverPit boosts the odds of patterns you'll encounter. The higher your luck, the greater the odds of a particular pattern appearing in a pull. Luck does not determine what symbol will be in the pattern, but only gives you greater odds of encountering better patterns. For example, if your Luck is 15, you'll have exceedingly great chances to see a Jackpot, but it doesn't guarantee it'll be made up of 7s (which is the most valuable symbol). So, Luck only gives you chances of encountering better patterns, but does not guarantee how many of those will appear, nor does it help you get better symbols more often. This last is reserved for appearance buffs, which are given via Red Button Charms, Phone Upgrades, and other Lucky Charms that grant guaranteed chances of seeing certain symbols in each pull.
